A payout is when Commet sends your available balance to your bank account.

> Payouts are available only in live organizations. Sandbox organizations can test charges and billing, but cannot verify an account or move funds out.

> Withdraw your available balance to your bank account in up to 7 business days.

## How it works

Verify your account, then request a payout of $10 or more from your available balance. Money reaches your bank account in up to 7 business days.

## The facts

- Minimum payout: **$10**.
- Money reaches your bank account in **up to 7 business days**.
- **No payout fee.**
- Account verification is required before your first payout.

Only your **Available** balance can be paid out. See the balance states in [Finance Overview](/docs/finance-overview).

## View in Dashboard

Navigate to **Payouts**. Add your bank accounts, then click **Withdraw funds** to request a payout from your available balance.

## Limitations

- Minimum payout is **$10**.
- Only the **Available** balance can be withdrawn — Pending funds cannot.
- Account verification is required before the first payout.
- Money reaches your bank account in **up to 7 business days**.
